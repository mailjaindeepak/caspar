Part 1 of 7 NCR Supply–Demand
NCR absorption has consistently outpaced supply since 2020
NCR unsold stock fell 51% between Q1 2020 and Q1 2025, from ~1.73 lakh units to ~84,500 units, as sales outpaced launches every year from 2020 to 2024 - peaking in 2023 with
65,600 units sold against 36,700 launched.
2024 marked the supply response: ~53,000 units launched, up 44% YoY. Momentum has since reversed - Q2 2026 launches fell 40% YoY to 11,205 units while sales fell only 6% to
13,365 units, so demand again exceeded new supply. Noida and Greater Noida saw the sharpest correction, with launches down 72% YoY to 2,140 units.
2020–22 and 2024–26 sales figures, and 2025 E / 2026 F, are consultant estimates derived from published quarterly data; 2023 launches and sales, and 2024 launches, are as reported by ANAROCK.
Source: ANAROCK, NCR Real Estate – A Beacon of Growth and Opportunity (April 2025) and Indian Residential Real Estate Sector Annual Report 2025; ANAROCK Q2 2026 NCR update; Equirus Securities June 2026 sector report via Business Standard

Part 2 of 7 Region Supply–Demand
Noida & Greater Noida: Absorption has
generally outpaced supply for last the 7 years
Supply only recovered from 2022 as Gaur, Godrej, ACE, CRC and Mahagun brought new group housing to market.
Absorption outpaced supply every year from 2019 to 2023, cutting unsold stock sharply - Noida's inventory overhang fell 72% between Q1 2020 and Q1 2025, and Greater Noida's by 56%,
the two steepest declines in NCR.
2024–25 marked the supply response, with launches peaking at ~19,700 units in 2025 E and briefly exceeding absorption. In 2026 demand again exceeds new supply, and overhang stays
inside the healthy 15–19 month band.

===== PAGE 63 =====
Part 2 of 4 Steps 1 & 2
NCR demand is expected to recover to 66,500 units by 2031,
with the premium share rising from 25% to 35% of the market
STEP 1 · Forecasting NCR-Level Demand & Supply
NCR residential supply and demand have been projected using historical growth rates, recent market events, the availability of developable land parcels and ongoing development momentum.
STEP 2 · Forecasting the Premium Segment Share within NCR
The share of premium housing within NCR has been estimated on the basis of recent launch activity, developer focus on premium projects, rising home owner affluence and the region's documented shift towards lifestyle-
led developments.
Anchor points: 2023 launches of 36,700 units and sales of 65,600 units, and 2024 launches of ~53,000 units, are as reported by ANAROCK. 2026 is annualised from H1 2026 actuals (24,836 launched, 24,105 sold). Intervening years
and all forecast years are consultant estimates.
The segment share anchor is 2025, where ANAROCK reports the ₹2–5 Cr bracket at 36% of NCR sales; the above-₹2.5 Cr share is derived at ~25%. Equirus reports 71% of Q1 2026 NCR sales above ₹1 Cr, confirming the direction
of travel.
Segment demand is forecast to grow faster than the overall market throughout the projection period, consistent with the region's structural premiumisation and the shift of developer launch focus to higher ticket bands.
Source: ANAROCK NCR reports (April 2025, Q2 2026); ANAROCK Indian Residential Real Estate Sector Annual Report 2025; Equirus Securities June 2026 via Business Standard; consultant projections

===== PAGE 64 =====
Part 3 of 4 Steps 3 & 4
The project needs 9–10% of the corridorʼs premium demand at launch,
decaying to under 2% - an aggregate capture of ~4.8% over five years
STEP 3 · Influence Region Premium Share - Greater Noida West
Greater Noida West's expected share of NCR premium demand has been derived by analysing its historical contribution to the region's premium market, its pricing depth, and its positioning as a maturing premium micro-market with
committed metro connectivity.
STEP 4 · Subject Property - Demand Capture
The site's demand share has been estimated by benchmarking comparable premium developments in the influence region and incorporating its unique value proposition as the corridor's first hospitality-led branded residence,
expected to command stronger appeal and absorption at launch.
Under the realistic scenario the project captures 9–10% of the influence region's premium and luxury demand in its launch year, decaying to under 2% by 2031 - reflecting both the launch-phase surge that branding delivers and the
reversion to fundamentals thereafter. Aggregate capture across the period is ~4.8% of forecast corridor demand. The optimistic scenario trades a flatter, slower absorption profile for a higher realisation. Both scenarios absorb the full 387-
unit programme; they differ in price and timeline, not in total volume.
Source: Consultant projections built on ANAROCK NCR data and the Autograph IM competitor set; corridor share trajectory calibrated to 99acres Greater Noida West price data and the peer set launch record 64

===== PAGE 72 =====
Part 3 of 3 NPV & Sensitivity
NPV of ₹5,362 Mn before land, and a residual land value roughly six
times GNIDA's 2026 reserve pricing - the headroom is substantial
Conclusion on financial viability
The project generates a net present value before land cost of ₹5,362 Mn under the realistic scenario and ₹5,430 Mn under the optimistic scenario - the two are close because the optimistic scenario's higher realisation is largely offset by its
slower absorption and consequently later cashflows. Both remain robustly positive across the full land-cost sensitivity range. The residual land value of ~₹3.10 lakh per sq. m sits far above the ₹36,500–48,300 per sq. m reserve pricing at
which the Greater Noida Authority offered group housing plots in its 2026 e-auction scheme, indicating substantial headroom. On this basis the hospitality-led branded residential proposition at Autograph is financially viable, subject to
