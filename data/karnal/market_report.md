## Locality price map

**Prime:** Alpha Intl City Sec 28–29 (₹9,000–12,200/sq ft, +178%/5yr); Sec 32/33 HSVP (₹80k–1.25 L/sq yd, S+4 corridor); established HSVP Sec 6/7/9, 4, 13, Urban Estate, Model Town (online rates stale — ground-truth with dealers); Sec 45/36/35/40/5 mid-to-upper.
**Non-prime/value:** Nilokheri, Gharaunda, Jhanjhari, Phoosgarh, Baldi, Gogripur.

## Active developers

- **National/regional:** Alpha Corp (strongest premium credential), Ansal API + Ansal Housing (legacy, weak balance sheet), Parsvnath (distressed), CHD (listed), Signature Global (listed, affordable positioning). No confirmed DLF/Omaxe/Godrej/Mahindra presence.
- **Local:** RCNP/JAP (40 yrs, Model Town), RAS, Slotco, Madhuban, Aegis Homes, Rajdarbar Builders, AB Greenvalleys, DAA Home Realtors, Metropole Buildtech, RBA Homes, Skyhigh Infraland.
- **Hospitality crossover:** Jewel Classic Hotels (Col. Manbeer Choudhary) — Noormahal Palace + Hotel Jewels; strongest local branded-residence mindset candidate.

## Luxury demand signals

1. **Noormahal Karnal became India's first Marriott Autograph Collection hotel (Apr 2026)** — 176 rooms, GT Road. Global luxury flag chose Karnal for an India-first debut.
2. Alpha City +178% in 5 yrs; ₹12.5 Cr top listing — premium absorption proven.
3. NCR builders buying Sec 32/33 HSVP plots for S+4 floors (Tribune).
4. Infra: Delhi–Amritsar–Katra expressway; Delhi–Panipat RRTS with proposed Karnal extension (Gharaunda, Madhuban, Karnal); rail junction; proposed airport; Smart City ~65%; collector rates +10–20% FY25-26.
5. **No true luxury group-housing or branded-residence supply exists — white space.**

## Demand context

District pop ~18.3 L; "rice bowl" wealth (basmati export hub), NRI corridor along GT Road, midway Delhi (~123 km) / Chandigarh (~130 km).

## Caveats

1. Verify "Godrej Plots Karnal" and "Global City" claims via RERA before crediting.
2. Prime HSVP kothi rates thinly evidenced online.
3. Best branded-residence platforms: Alpha Corp (scale) and Jewel Classic Hotels (mindset).
